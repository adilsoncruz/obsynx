# OBSYNX Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of OBSYNX pledge to make participation in our project and community a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, nationality, personal identity, or technical background.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
* Demonstrating empathy, patience, and kindness toward fellow contributors.
* Respecting differing viewpoints, architectural opinions, and technical approaches.
* Giving and gracefully accepting constructive technical feedback.
* Focusing on what is best for the overall reliability, performance, and developer experience of the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
* Harassment, trolling, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
* Publishing private information of others without explicit consent.
* Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional open-source community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (repositories, discussions, issue trackers, and pull requests) associated with OBSYNX.
